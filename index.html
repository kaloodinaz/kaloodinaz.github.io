Getting Started

What Are io Games?

io games are free, browser-based games that grew popular thanks to their pick-up-and-play mechanics and competitive edge. The name comes from the .io domain many of them originally launched on, but today it's shorthand for a whole genre - quick, social, no-download games you can jump into on a whim. This list stretches the idea a little further, mixing in Wordle-style word games and browser classics that share the same instant, no-fuss spirit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are io games?

io games are free browser games, usually multiplayer, that originally used the .io domain extension. They run instantly in any browser with no download or install, and typically feature simple controls with fast, competitive rounds.

Do I need to download anything to play?

No. Every game on this list runs directly in your browser tab. Just tap a game and it loads right away on desktop or mobile.

Are these games free to play?

Yes, all games featured here are free to play. Some may show optional ads, but there is no cost to start playing.

Can I play io games on my phone?

Most games on this page are built to be responsive and work well on phones and tablets, though a few are best experienced on a larger screen with a keyboard and mouse.

How often is the games list updated?

New titles are added regularly as fresh io games, Wordle spinoffs and browser classics are discovered, so it's worth checking back from time to time.
